# MotoHealth Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

MotoHealth is a private, local-first app for tracking a motorcycle. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## Data we collect

None. MotoHealth has no account, no backend and no analytics, and the developer receives no data from the app.

## Data stored on your device

Your bikes, odometer readings, fuel, service and expense entries, and paper expiry dates are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Backups you export are files you create and keep yourself.

## Optional AI features

If you choose to use the AI features, you add your own API key for OpenAI, Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude. When you start an AI action, the app sends the relevant bike details and records (make, model, year, riding style, and the logs needed for the analysis) directly from your phone to the provider you selected. The developer never sees this data. The provider's own privacy policy governs how it handles that data. Your API key is kept in the device's secure storage and is sent only to that provider. If you never add a key, no data leaves your phone.

## Notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device for expiring papers and due maintenance. No notification data is sent anywhere.

## Permissions

- Internet: only for the optional AI features.
- Notifications: reminders for papers and maintenance.
- Run at startup: restores scheduled reminders after a reboot.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
